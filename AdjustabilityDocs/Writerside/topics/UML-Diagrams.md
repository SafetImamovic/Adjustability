# UML Diagrams

## What are UML Diagrams?

[Wikipedia Article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Modeling_Language)

**UML (Unified Modeling Language)** diagrams are a standardized way to visualize
the design of a system. They provide a graphical representation of software elements,
making it easier to understand, design, and document software systems. UML diagrams
can be divided into two main categories: structural diagrams and behavioral diagrams.
