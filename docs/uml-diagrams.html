What are UML Diagrams?

Wikipedia Article

UML (Unified Modeling Language) diagrams are a standardized way to visualize the design of a system. They provide a graphical representation of software elements, making it easier to understand, design, and document software systems. UML diagrams can be divided into two main categories: structural diagrams and behavioral diagrams.
