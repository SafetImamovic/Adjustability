Adjustability

Have you ever found yourself frustrated by Minecraft's limitation of only allowing one mutually exclusive enchantment per item?

For instance, you can't have both Fortune and Silk Touch on a pickaxe or multiple types of Protection on your armor at the same time.

What if you could seamlessly switch between these enchantments without needing multiple tools or armor pieces?

Introducing Adjustability

Adjustability for Minecraft 1.20 is here to solve that problem.

This mod allows players to toggle between mutually exclusive enchantments on their items, giving you the flexibility to adapt to different tasks on the fly.

Whether you're mining diamonds and need Fortune or harvesting grass blocks with Silk Touch, Adjustability lets you do it all with just one tool or piece of armor.

Key Features

  • Toggle Enchantments: Switch between enchantments like Fortune, Silk Touch, and different types of protection as needed.

  • No Stacking: Enchantments do not stack; Adjustability provides a convenient way to toggle between them during gameplay.

  • Modpack Integration: Modpack developers can configure the mod with features like XP costs for toggling, enhancing gameplay customization.

Using Adjustability

Getting Started

  1. Installation: Download Adjustability from GitHub and install it into your Minecraft 1.20 instance using your preferred mod loader.

Using the Mod

  1. Enchanting Items: Enchant your item with mutually exclusive enchantments using the crafting table or enchanting table.

  2. Toggling Enchantments: In-game, use the toggle function (default key: TBD) to switch between available enchantments on the item.
